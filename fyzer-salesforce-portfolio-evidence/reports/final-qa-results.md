## Simulations labeling
Quote-to-Cash and PSA artifacts are **simulations** built on standard/custom objects and are labeled "(SIMULATION)" where they appear. They are not Salesforce CPQ, Revenue Cloud, or Certinia, and are never represented as such.

## Developer Edition limitations encountered
Knowledge was reported manually enabled by the org owner; verification is pending; entitlement/milestone engine does not complete milestones on closure (KI-1); record-triggered flows on delete unsupported (item 7); in-use SLA processes cannot be updated via metadata deploy (KI-2); Metric dashboard charts cannot source from tabular reports (documented substitution in the Executive dashboard); the legacy GPT-4o agent template carries a platform deprecation advisory (retires March 2027).
